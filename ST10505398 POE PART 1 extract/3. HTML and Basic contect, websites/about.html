About WALBOT

Quality laptop accessories built for real life

Our Story

WALBOT was born from a simple frustration—why should protecting your laptop cost a fortune?

The founder, a university student juggling late-night study sessions and a part-time job, noticed that quality laptop sleeves and accessories were either overpriced or poorly made. What started as a small side hustle, selling handmade laptop sleeves to classmates, quickly grew into a trusted online brand.

Today, WALBOT serves hundreds of customers across the country, offering durable, stylish, and affordable laptop accessories that actually last.

Our Mission

To equip every laptop owner with accessories that combine durability, style, and value—because your tech deserves better than cheap, flimsy gear.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted name in laptop accessories, known for quality that outlasts trends and customer service that feels like a friend helping a friend.
