## Project Overview

WALBOT is a fictional laptop accessories business based in Johannesburg, South Africa. The company sells durable, stylish, and affordable laptop sleeves, carry bags, screen protectors, keyboard covers, and charging accessories.

This website was built as part of a Portfolio of Evidence (PoE) to demonstrate skills in:

- HTML5 structure and semantics (Part 1)
- CSS3 styling and responsive design (Part 2)
- JavaScript functionality and form validation (Part 3)
- SEO best practices
- Version control using Git and GitHub

The site targets university students, remote workers, and business professionals who need quality laptop accessories without the premium price tag.

---

## Website Goals and Objectives

### Goals

- Increase online sales by 30% within six months
- Build brand trust through honest content and customer reviews
- Grow an email subscriber list for future promotions
- Educate customers through blog content about laptop care and accessories

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Monthly sales revenue and number of orders
- Website conversion rate (visitors to buyers)
- Organic traffic growth from search engines
- Email newsletter sign-up rate
- Average time spent on product pages
- Customer enquiry response time
